\begin{abstract}

    總體基因體學的分類學分類——即將每條定序短讀序列指派至正確的微生物分類單元——是微生物群落分析的核心計算挑戰。本論文系統性評估基因體基礎模型於總體基因體短讀序列分類的適用性，並以受控消融分離資料規模、骨幹深度、預訓練與 tokenization。我們以 Nucleotide Transformer v2（498M 參數）搭配低秩適應（LoRA）與注意力池化分類頭，作為一般用途基因體基礎模型的代表。在固定 NT-v2 非重疊 6-mer 架構下，訓練資料量主導準確度：屬層級 120 類由 500K 增至 5M 提升 7.76 個百分點（55.29\% 至 63.05\%），50M 達 67.07\%；再擴 $5\times$ 至 250M 僅 $+0.22$ 個百分點（67.29\%）。此飽和是 tokenizer 的上限而非資料的上限：同一批讀序、同一 $50\text{M}\to250\text{M}$ 區間，重疊 13-mer 的 MetaTransformer 由 87.5\% 續升至 98.7\%。先前以 29 層預訓練 NT-v2 對單層隨機初始化 Transformer 得到的 $+13.19$ 個百分點，把預訓練與深度混在一起。在相同 6-mer tokenizer、相同 50M 讀序、同一 \texttt{clean\_common} 100K 測試檔與反向互補測試時增強（RC TTA）下，從頭訓練的 1 / 8 / 16 層模型分別為 53.92\% / 65.44\% / 67.94\%，而 NT-v2 LoRA 為 67.08\%：深度本身即解釋 $+14.02$ 個百分點（1$\to$16 層），預訓練淨貢獻約為 0（16 層從頭訓練甚至高出 0.86 個百分點）。預訓練的可辯護效益出現在標籤稀缺時：同樣深度對齊的 16 層對照，5M 讀序上 NT-v2 為 63.05\%、從頭訓練為 54.11\%（$+8.94$ 個百分點；該 63.05\% 為內部驗證數字，見正文）。跨架構 tokenization 消融中，重疊 13-mer MetaTransformer 於相同 50M 讀序達 87.42\%，較 NT-v2 + LoRA（67.07\%）高 20.35 個百分點。同架構 16 層對照進一步顯示：hashed 13-mer（2~GiB）達 83.83\%，exact 13-mer 達 85.63\%；6-mer 即使加到 29 層也僅 69.52\%。物種層級 1,535 類上，per-genus 50M LoRA 於 oracle genus routing 達 29.5\% Top-1，預測 routing 降至 15.6\%；subgenus 路由為負結果。樣本層級在物種平衡隨機分割合成樣本中，67\% 單讀序列準確度可轉為相對豐度 Pearson $r = 0.993$，但屬層級豐度無法用來選方法（Kraken2+Bracken $r = 0.997$），真實多樣性社群泛化仍待驗證。本研究範圍為一般用途基因體基礎模型（NT-v2、DNABERT）；METAGENE-1 與 GenomeOcean 等以總體基因體預訓練的模型未經評估。結論是 tokenization 為主要瓶頸，深度可以彌補不良 tokenizer，預訓練提升的是資料效率而非 50M 上限。

\end{abstract}

\begin{abstract*}

    Metagenomic taxonomic classification---assigning each sequencing read to its source organism in the microbial taxonomy---is a fundamental computational challenge in microbiome analysis. This thesis evaluates genomic foundation models (GFMs) for short-read taxonomic classification through controlled ablations of data scale, backbone depth, pre-training, and tokenization. We take Nucleotide Transformer v2 (NT-v2, 498M parameters) with Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) and an attention-pooling head as the representative general-purpose GFM.

    Within the fixed NT-v2 non-overlapping 6-mer framework, training data volume dominates architecture and training tricks: a $10\times$ increase yields $+7.76$ percentage points (pp) in genus-level accuracy (55.29\% to 63.05\% on 120 genera at 5M reads), scaling further to 67.07\% at 50M, whereas technique ablations move accuracy by at most $\pm$0.5~pp. A further $5\times$ increase to 250M yields only $+0.22$~pp (67.29\%). That saturation is a property of the tokenizer, not of the data: trained on the same reads over the same $50\text{M}\to250\text{M}$ range, an overlapping 13-mer model scales from 87.5\% to 98.7\% genus Top-1. Reverse-complement test-time augmentation (RC TTA) improves accuracy by $+0.1$ to $+1.5$~pp. The previously reported $+13.19$~pp gap between 29-layer pre-trained NT-v2 and a one-layer randomly initialized Transformer at 50M conflates pre-training with depth. Holding the 6-mer tokenizer, 50M reads, attention-pooling head, and the same \texttt{clean\_common} 100K file fixed under RC TTA, from-scratch models score 53.92\% / 65.44\% / 67.94\% at 1 / 8 / 16 layers, against 67.08\% for NT-v2 LoRA: depth alone accounts for $+14.02$~pp ($1\to 16$ layers), and the net contribution of pre-training at this scale is $\approx 0$ (the 16-layer from-scratch model is $+0.86$~pp higher). A defensible pre-training benefit appears when labels are scarce: at matched 16-layer depth and 6-mer tokenization, NT-v2 reaches 63.05\% at 5M reads versus 54.11\% from scratch ($+8.94$~pp; the 63.05\% figure is the recorded internal-validation number, as disclosed in the main text).

    Cross-architecture tokenization ablations remain decisive: MetaTransformer with overlapping 13-mer tokenization, trained from scratch on the same 50M reads, reaches 87.42\% genus Top-1, $+20.35$~pp above NT-v2 + LoRA (67.07\%). A same-architecture 16-layer comparison on \texttt{clean\_common} RC TTA further shows that a hashed 13-mer (2~GiB embedding) reaches 83.83\% and an exact 13-mer 85.63\%, while a 29-layer from-scratch 6-mer reaches only 69.52\%. At the species level (1,535 classes), per-genus 50M LoRA classifiers under oracle-genus routing reach 29.5\% Top-1; predicted routing through the genus model lowers this to 15.6\%. Subgenus routing is a negative result. In synthetic species-balanced random-partition samples, 67\% per-read accuracy translates to Pearson $r = 0.993$ for relative abundance, but genus-rank abundance cannot choose a method (Kraken2+Bracken $r = 0.997$); evaluation on real and compositionally variable communities remains future work. The scope is general-purpose GFMs (NT-v2, DNABERT); metagenome-pre-trained models such as METAGENE-1 and GenomeOcean were not tested. Tokenization is the bottleneck; depth can compensate for a weak tokenizer; pre-training buys data efficiency, not a higher 50M ceiling.

\end{abstract*}
